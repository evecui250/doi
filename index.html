Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Everything you write stays on your iPhone.

What happens if I get a new iPhone?

Your journal is stored only on your iPhone. If iCloud Backup is on, it is included in your device backup and comes back when you restore that backup to a new iPhone. An in-app backup is planned for a future version.

I forgot my PIN.

On the unlock screen, choose the option to reset. This clears the decisions and PIN on this phone.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a decision from the home screen, or clear everything with Account › Privacy › Reset this device. Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored.

Can you read my journal?

No. Nothing you write is sent to us or anyone else — there are no servers involved.

常见问题

需要注册账户吗？

不需要。应用没有账户系统，你写下的一切都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。

换了新 iPhone 怎么办？

你的日记只保存在你的 iPhone 上。如果开启了 iCloud 云备份，它会包含在设备备份中，在新 iPhone 上恢复该备份后即可找回。应用内备份功能计划在未来版本中推出。

忘记密码怎么办？

在解锁页面选择重置。这会清除这台手机上的决定和密码。

如何删除我的数据？

在首页删除某个决定，或通过「账户 › 隐私 › 重置这台设备」清除全部内容。删除应用也会删除它保存的所有内容。

你们能看到我的日记吗？

不能。你写下的任何内容都不会发送给我们或任何其他人——整个过程不涉及任何服务器。
