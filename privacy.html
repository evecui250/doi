Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated October 6, 2026

Do I? is a decision-journal app. This explains, plainly, what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

Everything stays on your device

Every decision you create, every vote you cast, any note, mood or photo you add, and your settings (PIN, Face ID preference, backgrounds, reminder times) are stored only on your iPhone. This version of Do I? has no accounts and no servers: nothing you write is sent to us or anyone else.

We don't collect usage data, and there are no ads or tracking of any kind. If iCloud Backup is on, iOS includes the app's data in your device backup, under Apple's terms — we have no access to it.

If you email us feedback, we receive only what you choose to send.

Do I? is a tool for recording and reflecting on your own decisions — it doesn't give advice, and nothing in the app should be read as a recommendation. Any decision you make, and any action you take based on it, is entirely your own choice and your own responsibility.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee against loss, corruption, or unauthorized access, including from a security incident outside our control.

To the extent permitted by law, we aren't liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your use of the app, and our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount (if any) you've paid us in the twelve months before the claim arose. Nothing here limits liability for fraud, gross negligence, or anything else the law doesn't allow us to disclaim.

Your choices

• Delete a decision — removes it from your device.
• Reset this device — in Account › Privacy; clears every decision and your PIN on this phone.
• Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored.

Do I? is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

最近更新：2026年10月6日

「问己」是一款决定日记类应用。以下将简单说明：哪些信息只留在你的手机上，哪些会离开手机、以及原因。

所有内容都留在你的设备上

你创建的每一个决定、每一次投票、添加的记录、心情或照片，以及你的设置（密码、面容 ID 偏好、背景、提醒时间），都只保存在你的 iPhone 上。这个版本的「问己」没有账户，也没有服务器：你写下的任何内容都不会发送给我们或任何其他人。

我们不收集使用数据，应用中也没有广告或任何形式的追踪。如果你开启了 iCloud 云备份，iOS 会按照 Apple 的条款将本应用的数据包含在设备备份中——我们无法访问这些数据。

如果你通过邮件向我们发送反馈，我们只会收到你主动发送的内容。
