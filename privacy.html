Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated October 6, 2026

Do I? is a decision-journal app. This explains, plainly, what stays on your phone, what leaves it, and why.

What stays on your device

Every decision you create, every vote you cast, any photo or drawing you attach, and your app settings (PIN, Face ID preference, paper background, reminder times) are stored locally on your phone. None of it is sent anywhere unless you turn on the optional feature below.

To know whether the app is actually being used, it sends a few anonymous usage counts: that the app was opened, a decision was created or resolved, or a vote was cast — with a random ID generated by the app and the time. Never what you wrote, your name, or your Apple ID. If you send feedback from Account, we receive the message you write. There are no ads or ad-tracking SDKs in this app, and nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Optional cloud backup

Signing in with Apple is entirely optional. If you choose to sign in, your decisions, votes, and their photos are backed up to our cloud database (currently Supabase) so you can restore them on a new device or after reinstalling. This is the only case where your journal content leaves your phone.

What we store: an app-specific account identifier (from Apple, not your Apple ID email unless you choose to share it, and never your real name); your decisions, votes, comments, and any attached photos; and your per-decision preferences so a restore looks the way you left it.

Your question, comments, moods, answer labels, and any attached photos are end-to-end encrypted before they ever leave your device — the encryption key lives in your device's Keychain and, if enabled, syncs through your own iCloud Keychain, never through our servers. This means we can't read or view that content even if we wanted to.

Signing out or deleting your account removes access to this backup going forward. Deleting a decision on your device also deletes its backed-up copy.

Third parties

We use Supabase to host the optional cloud backup and handle authentication (Apple's Sign in with Apple). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

If Do I? expands to serve users in mainland China, part of this infrastructure may run on a China-based cloud provider instead of Supabase, under the same principles described here. We'll update this page before that happens.

A note on backend access: other users never see your identity or your private journal. As the developer, we retain administrative access to the backend database that hosts backup data — the same way any app's operator can access their own servers. As described above, your question, comments, answer labels, and any attached photos are encrypted before they reach that database, so this access doesn't extend to viewing them — only to metadata like dates and account structure. We only look at what we can access to investigate abuse reports, fix bugs, or respond to a support request you send us; it's not reviewed routinely or used for any other purpose.

Where data is stored, and your rights

The optional cloud backup and the anonymous usage counts are stored with Supabase, on servers in Sydney, Australia.

If you are in the EU, EEA or UK: we process the backup because you asked for it by signing in (performance of a contract), and the usage counts and feedback on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. Transfers to Australia are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses in Supabase's data processing agreement.

Wherever you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used — most of this you can do yourself in the app (see "Your choices"), or contact us below. We keep backup data until you delete it or your account. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

隐私政策与条款

最近更新：2026年10月6日

「问己」是一款决定日记类应用。以下将简单说明：哪些信息只留在你的手机上，哪些会离开手机、以及原因。

留在你设备上的信息

你创建的每一个决定、每一次投票、附带的照片或涂鸦，以及应用设置（密码、Face ID 偏好、纸张背景、提醒时间），都保存在你的手机本地。除非你主动开启下面的可选功能，否则不会上传到任何地方。

为了解应用是否真的有人在用，应用会发送少量匿名使用计数：打开了应用、创建或完成了一个决定、投了一票——附带一个由应用随机生成的标识和时间。绝不包含你写下的内容、你的姓名或 Apple ID。如果你在「账户」中发送意见反馈，我们会收到你写的内容。应用中没有广告或广告追踪 SDK，也不会用于在其他应用或网站上追踪你。

可选的云端备份

登录 Apple 账户完全是可选的。如果你选择登录，你的决定、投票记录及其照片会备份到我们的云端数据库（目前是 Supabase），以便你在新设备或重新安装应用后恢复数据。这是你的日记内容唯一会离开手机的情况。

登录后我们会存储：一个应用专属账户标识（不是你的 Apple ID 邮箱，除非你主动选择分享，也绝不包含你的真实姓名）；与该账户关联的决定、投票、评论及附带照片；以及你的每个决定的偏好设置，以便恢复后与你离开时的样子一致。

你的问题、评论、心情、选项标签，以及任何附带的照片，在离开你的设备前就已进行端到端加密——加密密钥保存在你设备的钥匙串中，如果你开启了该功能，还会通过你自己的 iCloud 钥匙串同步，绝不会经过我们的服务器。这意味着即使我们想看，也无法读取或查看这些内容。

退出登录或删除账户后，今后将无法再访问该备份。在设备上删除某个决定，也会同步删除其云端备份副本。

第三方服务

我们使用 Supabase 承载可选的云端备份并处理身份验证（Apple 的「使用 Apple 登录」）。我们不会出售你的数据，也不会与广告商或数据经纪公司共享数据。

如果「问己」未来扩展至中国大陆用户，部分基础设施可能会改用中国大陆的云服务商，而非 Supabase，但仍遵循本页所述的相同原则。我们会在变更前更新本页面。

关于后台访问权限的说明：其他用户永远无法看到你的身份或你的私人日记内容。作为开发者，我们保留对承载备份数据的后台数据库的管理权限——这与任何应用的运营者可以访问自己服务器的方式相同。如上文所述，你的问题、评论、选项标签，以及任何附带的照片，在到达该数据库前已被加密，因此这项权限并不包括查看这些内容——仅限于日期、账户结构等元数据。我们仅在调查举报、修复问题，或回应你主动发起的支持请求时才会查看我们能够访问到的数据，不会用于日常巡查或其他任何目的。

数据存储位置与你的权利

可选的云端备份与匿名使用计数存储在 Supabase，服务器位于澳大利亚悉尼。

如果你位于欧盟、欧洲经济区或英国：我们处理备份数据，是因为你通过登录主动要求使用该功能（履行合同）；处理使用计数和意见反馈，是基于我们维持应用正常运行并加以改进的正当利益。向澳大利亚传输数据受 Supabase 数据处理协议中的标准合同条款保护。

无论你身在何处，你都可以要求访问、更正、导出或删除你的数据，或反对我们的使用方式——其中大部分你可以直接在应用内完成（见「你的选择」），也可以通过下方方式联系我们。备份数据会一直保留，直到你删除它或删除账户。如果你位于欧盟、欧洲经济区或英国，你还可以向当地的数据保护机构投诉。
